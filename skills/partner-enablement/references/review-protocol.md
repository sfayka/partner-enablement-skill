# Independent course review protocol

## Inputs

Give reviewers the same versioned raw Markdown, stage, brief, governing design standard, source references and overrides. Do not seed first passes with another reviewer's findings or the author's preferred verdict. No external publication or product modification is authorized by a review task.

Use four functions, disclosing combined roles. Prefer separate agents for full reviews. If unavailable, disclose sequential passes and limited independence.

## Assignments

**Instructional design:** inspect prior knowledge, sequencing, load, worked examples, fading guidance, practice, diagnostic feedback, corrections, retrieval, spacing and transfer. At blueprint stage inspect concrete commitments; at manuscript stage cite actual content.

**Assessment:** inspect objective coverage, authentic evidence, AI-permitted claims, item quality, feedback, mandatory outcomes, analytic anchors, fairness, equivalent forms, retries, timing and assessor capacity. Aggregate scores cannot compensate for essential failed outcomes.

**Instructor handoff:** inspect baseline competence, preparation, explanation completeness, cues, demos, expected results, reset, fallback, misconceptions, coaching, debrief and escalation. Identify author-dependent gaps; never invent actual delivery trial results.

**Technical integrity and consistency:** inspect source authority, procedures, dependencies, identifiers, failure semantics, recovery, unverified assumptions, fixtures and cross-artifact contradictions. Code inspection is not execution evidence. Trace changes through the manifest.

## Finding format

Return ID, reviewer function, criterion, content location, severity, evidence, practical consequence, required correction and recheck condition. A blocker violates a mandatory current-stage criterion. An advisory is optional improvement. End with PASS or BLOCKED for the named stage and list unperformed external validations.

## Revision

Consolidate duplicates without losing evidence. Revise source, record dispositions and have originating reviewers verify corrections. Recheck dependencies. Resolve disagreement against sources and governing rules, never majority vote. Missing mandatory evidence remains a blocker even if reviewers overlooked it.

After three unresolved cycles report the blocked requirement and dependency. Do not ask the human to decide instructional science. Explicit policy exceptions are possible but cannot be called an unqualified pass.

Record stage, source version, reviewer functions, criterion evidence, findings, revisions, rechecks and external validation status. Zero unresolved current-stage blockers is required before human suitability review. Technical execution, actual instructor handoff and learner pilots remain separate evidence gates.
