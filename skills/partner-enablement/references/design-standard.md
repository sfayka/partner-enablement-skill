# Partner enablement design standard

Version 0.1 | November 16 2026

## Purpose and responsibility

Create technical partner enablement covering sales, implementation, integration, and support through one workflow. Enable a capable facilitator with declared baseline competence and light familiarity with the course to prepare, teach, recover from predictable failures, and assess consistently without the author. Own instructional design decisions and alignment checks. Ask humans to review suitability, factual uncertainties, and deliverability, never to determine whether instructional principles were followed.

Apply this standard with the skill instructions. This specification is not proof that any course passed validation.

## Authority and overrides

Use verified product documentation and execution evidence for product facts; approved organization terminology for names and commercial rules; the instructional requirements below for course design. Use Diataxis for documentation purpose and Google technical writing guidance for clarity. Treat learning research as scoped evidence, not a reason to impose contradictory universal prescriptions.

Record explicit course-specific overrides in the brief. Respect the user's decisions; identify implications for learning or certification claims. Never silently waive a mandatory criterion. If an override prevents alignment, accurately report an exception rather than a clean pass. Never invent capabilities, commands, tests, human reviews, pilots, or approvals.

## Brief and scope

Establish learner roles, job tasks, prior knowledge, instructor baseline competence, delivery mode, environment, sources, objectives, intended artifacts, time budget, learning or certification mode, and approved exceptions. Ask only material unresolved questions. Set observable objectives and evidence requirements before drafting lessons. Distinguish a role-spanning foundation from advanced competence in each profession.

For sales require fit, discovery, defensible value and limitations, objections, and implementation handoff where relevant. For implementation require prerequisites, architecture, authentication, working examples, verification and recovery. For support require symptoms, diagnostic evidence, remediation, verification and escalation. Combine applicable requirements; do not create three separate skills.

## Mandatory instructional construction

Require the following in every full course. Show concrete evidence, not statements of intention. At blueprint stage specify activity, intervention, timing, and later verification; at manuscript stage inspect actual content and answer keys. An isolated reference or how-to uses only applicable requirements, with an explicit scope reason.

1. Define readiness through an observable preflight. Give missed prerequisites a preparation route, retest, and completion consequence. Teach new course concepts rather than quietly reclassifying them as prerequisites.
2. Preview outcomes, relevance, and successful performance. Build a useful conceptual model before introducing dependent details. Select only content serving a task, decision, prerequisite, or essential explanation.
3. Manage cognitive load through short explain, demonstrate, do, and check cycles. Present related explanation beside the action or visual it explains. Segment complicated procedures; remove irrelevant decoration and repeated prose. Preserve essential explanation rather than stripping content for brevity.
4. Provide complete worked examples with reasoning and expected results. Follow with partially guided practice, then a changed scenario with reduced guidance. Use contrasting examples when learners must distinguish conditions.
5. Assess during learning. For each major misconception provide a diagnostic prompt, expected answer, interpretation of plausible wrong answers, corrective explanation, and a recheck. Gather responses from every learner, not only volunteers. Specify when to reteach, coach individually, or defer optional enrichment.
6. Require relevant hands-on or decision practice, explicit success criteria, useful feedback, troubleshooting, cleanup, and instructor solutions. Watching the instructor does not establish performance. Pair practice must not substitute for individual certification evidence.
7. Recap concepts through learner retrieval and application, not repeated slide text alone. Revisit earlier ideas later in the session. Include a defined spaced follow-up task with feedback; course-specific alternatives must explain how reinforcement is achieved. Do not claim a universal scientifically optimal interval.
8. Test transfer with a scenario that changes meaningful details without introducing untaught requirements. Require reasoning and verification, not copied outputs alone.
9. Align each objective to explanation, worked example, practice, feedback, and final assessment. Quizzes must concern taught material, use plausible diagnostic alternatives, and include rationales. Practical assessment establishes task performance; a quiz cannot replace it.

## Assessment defaults

Allow documentation and approved AI tools. State tool availability, data rules, collaboration limits, and assistance disclosure. Provide equivalent tool access or an alternative; do not grade paid model quality. Never equate an AI-assisted submission with proof of unaided competence. Use an explicit assisted-performance certification claim and observed or recorded task evidence with accountable explanations and adaptation.

Separate coached learning from certification. Default quiz pass is 80 percent. Require separate practical pass with observable analytic rubric anchors and mandatory checks for critical objectives; no aggregate score may compensate for a mandatory failed check. Define objective performance requirements independently of integrity concerns. Use a documented clarification process for disputed evidence. Explain feedback timing and remediation; retries use equivalent forms with comparable complexity and evidence requirements. Define assessor capacity, timing, accommodations, interruptions, and certification version. Review reassessment needs when assessed product behavior materially changes. These thresholds are house defaults, not research findings.

## Material and instructor requirements

Use Markdown as canonical content. Maintain objective, activity, checkpoint, fault, evidence, and assessment IDs plus product contract and artifact versions in one manifest. Reconcile accepted review changes into source and regenerate all affected assets.

For live courses normally produce a student DOCX workbook, a standalone participant reference PDF, an instructor PPTX with substantive notes, and a separate instructor DOCX guide with solutions and assessment answers. Produce only applicable assets for self-paced courses or individual requests. Adapt content to each purpose; do not export one manuscript unchanged into all formats. Keep instructor-only material inaccessible in learner files, including hidden notes and metadata.

Specify bounded instructor preparation, prerequisites, rehearsal, and readiness checks. Notes must supply teaching intent, essential explanation, examples, timing, transitions, prompts, anticipated answers and misconceptions, demonstrations, expected results, fallback, exercise facilitation, coaching boundaries, debrief, and escalation when applicable. Label mandatory teaching points and allowed instructor discretion. No author-dependent gap is acceptable. An AI facilitator is a possible future consumer, not a supported delivery mode until tested.

## Prehuman review council

Use independent reviewers for instructional design, assessment, instructor handoff, and technical integrity with cross-artifact consistency. Run a full council for new courses, certifications, and major revisions. Combine roles only when disclosed; retain distinct criteria. For small edits use targeted review plus impact checks. If separate agents are unavailable, run sequential role passes and disclose the weaker independence; never invent council agreement.

Give reviewers the raw source, stage, applicable criteria, and authoritative references. Do not seed them with the author's preferred verdict or other reviewers' findings before their first pass. Require each finding to contain ID, criterion, content location, severity, evidence, consequence, and required correction. A blocker prevents the current stage gate. An advisory suggests improvement without violating a mandatory criterion.

Author revisions must address every blocker with evidence. Re-review revised content and affected dependencies. Resolve disagreements through the governing criterion and sources, not majority vote. After three unresolved cycles stop and report the concrete blocked requirement; do not lower the standard or ask the human to supply instructional expertise. Distinguish unresolved product facts from instructional defects.

## Stage gates and evidence

1. Blueprint gate: require concrete design commitments for all principles, coherent objectives and evidence, feasible provisional timing, readiness and recovery plans, assessment policy, instructor preparation, and declared verification work. Do not demand nonexistent finished assets or claim course compliance from a blueprint pass.
2. Manuscript gate: inspect the actual complete Markdown lessons, examples, exercises, prompts, feedback, answer keys, rubrics, and instructor directions. Require evidence for each mandatory criterion and zero unresolved blockers across reviewers before creating the human review copy.
3. Human suitability gate: provide DOCX and a concise council disposition. Ask whether people can use and deliver it, whether scenarios fit the partners, and what delivery problems remain. Human approval does not establish instructional alignment or complete technical execution. Repeat affected audits after human changes.
4. Final production gate: after human approval generate requested final formats. Render and inspect each artifact, verify notes and answer separation, cross-check manifest consistency, and resolve conversion defects. Record actual execution evidence, representative learner pilot and nonauthor instructor handoff results for a delivery-ready claim. If these have not happened, report the package as unvalidated for delivery, even when its instructional manuscript passed.

Never ask the human to approve avoidable defects. Record stage, source version, reviewers, criterion evidence, findings, dispositions, recheck outcomes, exceptions, and unperformed external validations. Consensus alone is not evidence. The skill owns design alignment; humans own suitability, observed usability and release authorization.

## Source basis

Implement these sources through the rules above; do not substitute citations for application. Retain section-level traceability in audit records, without burdening learner materials with academic commentary.

- The Carpentries Instructor Training, Building Skill With Practice, Memory and Cognitive Load, Building Skill With Feedback, Preparing to Teach: https://carpentries.github.io/instructor-training/ . Practical teaching guidance supporting segmentation, active practice, diagnostic checks and preparation.
- Diataxis, Tutorials, How-to Guides, Reference, Explanation: https://diataxis.fr/ . Documentation purpose and architecture; not a comprehensive assessment or certification standard. Keep guided learning distinct from independent assessment.
- Google Technical Writing, Audience, Sample Code, Procedures: https://developers.google.com/tech-writing and https://developers.google.com/style/procedures . Audience, clarity and executable task communication.
- National Academies, How People Learn II, 2018: https://doi.org/10.17226/24783 . Research synthesis supporting attention to prior knowledge, context, practice and transfer; not a fixed course recipe.
- Benassi, Overson and Hakala, Applying Science of Learning in Education, 2014: https://teachpsych.org/ebooks/asle2014/ . Relevant chapters include worked examples, feedback, test-enhanced learning, multimedia, spacing and interleaving. Apply within stated scope rather than claiming all findings transfer unchanged to every workplace.
- Cathy Moore, action mapping resources: https://blog.cathy-moore.com/book-resources/ . Performance analysis and realistic activities. GitLab partner enablement and Insight Partners handbook are practitioner examples only, not governing research.

The exact teaching sequence, document formats, review council, pass scores, preparation estimates and approval workflow are explicit operating choices. Evaluate their effectiveness with actual course use.
