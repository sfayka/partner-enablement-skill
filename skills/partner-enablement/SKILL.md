# Partner enablement

Own instructional design quality. Use human review for suitability, practical delivery, factual questions needing a subject expert, and release authorization. Do not ask the human to establish instructional alignment.

Read [design-standard.md](references/design-standard.md) before designing or auditing a full course and [review-protocol.md](references/review-protocol.md) before a review gate. These are binding instructions. For an individual asset read applicable requirements and record why course-only criteria do not apply.

## Establish the brief

Define learner roles and baseline, instructor baseline, tasks, sources, observable objectives, delivery mode, environment, learning versus certification mode, time budget and outputs. Ask only material missing questions. Record explicit overrides and their effects on learning or certification claims. Use authoritative product sources and execution evidence for technical facts; never invent capabilities or tests.

Activate applicable sales, implementation and support requirements in one workflow. Distinguish a role-spanning foundation from advanced competence in each profession.

Before drafting, build a role consistency matrix: included role, actual job task, justified prerequisites, taught capability, practice, assessment and certification claim. Challenge each role's inclusion. A skill supporting several roles does not imply one course must teach them all. Exclude unsupported roles or provide accessible prerequisites and genuinely differentiated paths. Never justify developer-level requirements merely by declaring a common foundation. Walk the course as a minimum-ready learner in every included role and resolve irrelevant demands and hidden knowledge before council review.

## Build in the principles

Require readiness checks with preparation and remediation; sequenced conceptual explanations; complete reasoned examples; short explain/demo/do/check cycles; gradually reduced guidance; diagnostic feedback with correction and rechecks; later retrieval and spaced reinforcement; realistic transfer; and objective-aligned assessment.

Use preview, explanation, demonstration, practice, recap and assessment to organize courses. Do not repeat identical prose mechanically or inflate a reference sheet into a course. Map each objective to instruction, example, practice, feedback and assessment evidence. Preserve practice and recovery time.

Apply the instructional sequence at lesson level: preview coverage and takeaways, explain, demonstrate, learner application, recap, and applicable assessment. Choose an appropriate exercise for the job task; not every lesson needs a separate lab or scored quiz. Preserve course-level assessment coverage. Immediately before a complex dependent concept, retrieve the needed foundational concept, explain the connection, diagnose gaps and reteach before continuing.

At manuscript stage, inspect actual content. A citation or assertion that a principle is followed cannot satisfy it. Keep guided learning distinct from independent assessment.

## Assess responsibly

Allow documentation and approved AI by default. State collaboration, tool access, disclosure and data rules. Assess accountable assisted task performance through verified results, explanation and adaptation; never claim unaided mastery from assisted submissions.

Default quiz pass is 80 percent with a separate practical pass. Require observable rubric anchors and mandatory outcomes that cannot be compensated by points. Define remediation, equivalent retries, feedback, assessor capacity, equal working allowances, accommodations, interruptions, and versioning. These are policy defaults, not scientific constants.

## Audit before human review

Maintain canonical Markdown and a shared manifest of objectives, activities, checkpoints, product versions, fixtures, evidence, assessment forms and artifact status.

Use independent review functions for new courses, certifications and major revisions: instructional design; assessment; instructor handoff; technical integrity and consistency. Follow the protocol. Reviewers inspect raw content separately first. Every finding needs criterion, location, evidence, consequence and correction. Revise and recheck affected content and dependencies. Resolve disagreements through scoped sources and explicit rules, not a vote.

Require every reviewer to cross-check the role consistency matrix against actual source content and challenge the author's audience framing. Presence checks alone cannot pass. A role-prerequisite-task-assessment mismatch blocks the stage. Human review is a last line of defense: when it catches a mandatory defect, supersede the affected pass, correct the content and failed review rule, add a reusable regression case, and rerun affected reviews before asking the human again.

Use targeted review plus impact checks for small edits. Disclose sequential passes if independent agents are unavailable; never invent a council pass. Stop after three unresolved revision cycles and report the blocked criterion without lowering the standard or asking the human to supply instructional expertise.

## Enforce stage gates

1. **Blueprint:** inspect concrete commitments, objectives and evidence, readiness, recovery, provisional timing, assessments and instructor preparation. Pass with zero design blockers; mark finished content and execution pending. Human review concerns proposed suitability and feasibility.
2. **Markdown manuscript:** inspect actual explanations, examples, labs, diagnostic prompts, corrective feedback, retrieval, transfer, questions, solutions, rubrics and teaching directions. Require evidence for every applicable criterion and zero blockers before creating the Word review copy. Blueprint approval cannot satisfy this gate.
3. **Human suitability:** provide DOCX with version, status, specific review questions and concise audit disposition. Reconcile feedback into Markdown and repeat affected audits. The owner need not adjudicate learning science.
4. **Final production:** after human approval generate applicable formats; render and inspect pages and slides, notes, accessibility, consistency and answer separation. Record actual technical execution, nonauthor instructor trial and learner pilot evidence before claiming delivery readiness. Otherwise report outstanding validation accurately.

## Enable transferable delivery

Provide bounded instructor preparation, explanations and reasoning, demonstrations, expected results, prompts and answers, misconceptions, coaching boundaries, debrief, recovery and escalation. Separate essential teaching points from optional discretion. A facilitator meeting baseline prerequisites must not need author help to fill gaps. Verify through a real nonauthor trial; do not claim AI delivery support until tested.

For live courses normally produce student DOCX workbook, standalone participant PDF, instructor PPTX with substantive speaker notes, and separate instructor DOCX guide with solutions and assessment keys. Tailor each artifact to its purpose. Use suitable authoring tools and their verification workflows. Generate only applicable outputs and keep instructor answers out of learner files, including hidden notes.

Report current stage, passed checks, unresolved issues, unperformed validations and next human decision. Always distinguish instructional alignment, technical verification, suitability approval and delivery readiness.
